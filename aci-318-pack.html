QUESTIONS

Before you buy

Which load combinations does the pack use?+

The sheet works to ACI 318-19's standard strength-design load combinations. If your project uses a different governing combination set, get in touch before buying and this can be adjusted.

Are the sheets suitable for submission to a checking engineer?+

They're built to show every step of the calculation with its section reference, which is what most checkers ask for — but you remain responsible for verifying the output against your own project's specific requirements.

Can I use one pack on more than one project?+

Yes. The licence is per user, not per project — use it on as many jobs as you like.

Do you offer a bundle with the Eurocode 2 pack?+

Not yet — Eurocode 2 and ACI 318 are sold separately for now. Get in touch if you need both and would like them priced together.
