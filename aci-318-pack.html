Built to the parts of 318-19 that changed. Where a member carries no shear reinforcement — slabs and footings — ACI 318-19 replaced the familiar 2√f'c with an equation that depends on the reinforcement ratio and carries the size effect factor λs. These sheets apply the current rule, so they will flag a footing that a pre-2019 calculation would have passed.
